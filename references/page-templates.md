# Page Templates

## Cover

Use for the first page.

Required elements:

- Dark-blue full-page background.
- WuXi AppTec logo.
- Large report title.
- Date and team name.
- Optional market flag or market cue.
- Large angled brand graphic or V-shaped cutout.
- Optional human or healthcare image as secondary visual.

## Table Of Contents

Use at chapter starts and major transitions.

Required elements:

- Left vertical dark-blue block or trapezoid.
- Large Chinese `目录` and small `CONTENTS`.
- Numbered horizontal section bars.
- Active chapter in blue, inactive chapters in gray.
- WuXi logo at lower right.

## Section Cover

Use before target groups or appendix.

Required elements:

- Full dark-blue background.
- Centered or right-of-center section title.
- Short bullet list of included subsections.
- WuXi logo near lower right.

## Insight Page

Use for market, industry, or strategic findings.

Structure:

- Conclusion-first title.
- Short subtitle explaining implication.
- 2-4 modules with dark-blue headers.
- One dominant chart, map, ecosystem graphic, or matrix.
- Side box or bottom strip for insight, implication, or caveat.
- Source line at bottom.

Good for:

- R&D forecast.
- MNC footprint.
- Industry ecosystem.
- Challenge summary.
- BD implication.

## Funnel Methodology

Use when explaining target selection.

Structure:

- Large funnel or narrowing process in the center.
- Universe count, screened count, high-potential count, shortlist count.
- Left side: criteria or data inputs.
- Right side: output definitions.
- Bottom or side note: how counts were derived.

Rule: use real counts from source data. If counts are placeholders, label them.

## Target Profile

Use for biotech, enabler, VC, or strategic partner pages.

Required blocks:

- Organization logo and intro.
- Platform/product/service description.
- Financing, partnership, or milestone timeline.
- Core capability.
- Key stakeholders or likely relationship owner.
- Potential WuXi entry point.
- Risk, uncertainty, or source needs.

Layout:

- White background.
- Top active section tab.
- 3-column or 4-block grid.
- Dark-blue module headers.
- Company logo at top right when available.
- Bottom brand bar.

## Key Stakeholder Grid

Use for people mapping.

Required fields:

- Name.
- Role.
- Organization.
- Photo if available.
- Career background.
- Scientific, operating, or investing relevance.
- Relationship clue or public contact route.
- LinkedIn or source indicator when available.

Common layouts:

- 2-column dense biography layout for 4-6 people.
- 4-card grid for shorter bios.
- Single row of stakeholders embedded inside target profile when only 2-3 names matter.

Do not guess identities from blurry names or photos.

## VC / Enabler Map

Use for ecosystem access and investor positioning.

Possible visuals:

- Positioning matrix.
- Portfolio table.
- Ecosystem relationship map.
- Logo map.
- Before/after institutional relationship diagram.

Required analysis:

- Institution role.
- Focus area.
- Portfolio or network reach.
- Fit with WuXi needs.
- Contact path.
- Trigger event or monitoring cue.

## Appendix Table

Use for long lists and monitoring logs.

Recommended columns:

- Entity.
- Type.
- Geography.
- Focus.
- Relevance.
- Priority.
- Rationale.
- Source.
- Last checked date.

Keep appendix pages readable, but density can be higher than main-story pages.
