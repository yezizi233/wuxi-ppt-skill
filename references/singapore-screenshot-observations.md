## Inferences

- The likely business question is not simply "what is the Singapore market", but "how should WuXi use Singapore as an APAC biopharma BD hub and target-entry point".
- The likely audience is WuXi internal BD, SMG, strategy, or management readers.
- The deck follows a consulting arc: macro context, industry opportunity and constraints, target shortlist, people-level BD pathway, and appendix traceability.
- The profile pages are designed for comparison across targets, not for standalone storytelling.
- The visual system is a reusable template family rather than one-off slide design.
- The most reusable pattern is "stable brand skeleton plus high-density evidence modules".
