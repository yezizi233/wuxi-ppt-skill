# Storyline Blueprint

## Default Deck Arc

Use this arc for WuXi-style biopharma market understanding and target landscaping decks:

1. Executive Summary
2. Country / Market Overview
3. Industry Insights
4. Target Profile
5. Target Enabler / VC
6. Appendix

## Executive Summary

Purpose: tell management what matters, why it matters now, and where BD should act.

Required content:

- Market role and strategic relevance.
- Demand drivers for WuXi services.
- Main opportunity pools.
- Key constraints or risks.
- Priority action areas.

## Country / Market Overview

Purpose: explain why the market has structural relevance.

Typical evidence:

- National positioning.
- Government strategy and public funding.
- Biomedical R&D expenditure or life-science investment.
- Cluster and infrastructure map.
- MNC or anchor institution presence.
- Talent, capital, clinical, and regulatory conditions.

Recommended logic:

`national strategy -> ecosystem formation -> cross-border relevance -> BD entry rationale`

## Industry Insights

Purpose: show the sector-level opportunity and constraints before moving to named targets.

Typical evidence:

- R&D or outsourcing demand.
- MNC local activity.
- Biotech ecosystem maturity.
- Platform, modality, or therapeutic-area concentration.
- Funding and partnership patterns.
- Bottlenecks: capital, clinical resources, talent, manufacturing, commercialization.

Recommended logic:

`industry momentum -> unmet service need -> opportunity segments -> constraints that shape target selection`

## Target Profile

Purpose: convert market opportunity into actionable organizations and people.

Typical sequence:

- Landscaping universe.
- Screening and ranking.
- Shortlist.
- Target company profiles.
- Key stakeholder pages.
- BD entry-point or next-step page.

Recommended funnel:

`160+ landscape -> 60+ relevant entities -> 30+ screened/ranked -> 10+ priority targets`

Numbers should be replaced by real counts from the user's dataset. Do not invent them.

## Target Enabler / VC

Purpose: map non-company routes into the ecosystem.

Typical entities:

- Government-linked accelerators.
- Translational research platforms.
- National research institutes.
- Venture builders.
- Local and overseas VCs active in the market.
- Strategic investors.

Recommended logic:

`ecosystem gatekeeper -> portfolio or network reach -> relationship pathway -> WuXi BD value`

## Appendix

Purpose: provide traceability, long lists, maps, and monitoring material without overloading the main story.

Typical pages:

- Bio-cluster and MNC distribution map.
- Full target list.
- Investor monitor list.
- Source log.
- Methodology detail.
- Data tables.

## Slide Title Rule

Use conclusion-first titles. Examples:

- "Singapore's national survival logic positions it as an APAC connection hub"
- "MNC investment and maturing local ecosystem jointly strengthen CRO outsourcing demand"
- "Capital, clinical resources, and talent remain the key constraints on ecosystem scaling"
- "Target screening narrows the landscape from broad universe to actionable BD shortlist"

Avoid neutral titles like "Market Overview" on analysis pages unless it is a chapter divider.
