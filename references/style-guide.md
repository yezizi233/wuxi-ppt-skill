# WuXi-Style Visual System

## Design Intent

Use a restrained, high-density consulting style. The deck should feel like an internal BD and strategy working document: fast to scan, easy to compare across targets, and grounded in evidence. It should not feel like a marketing landing page, public brochure, or decorative pitch deck.

## Palette

Use a tight blue-gray palette:

| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| Navy | Main brand blocks, chapter covers, footer bars, primary title accents |
| Royal blue | Module headers, active section tabs, selected steps |
| Light cyan | Small accents, angled footer wedge, secondary chart highlight |
| Cool gray | Inactive tabs, table fills, non-current chapters, separators |
| White | Main content background |
| Black / deep gray | Body text |

Avoid introducing unrelated saturated colors. Use company logos in original color when needed, but keep the slide structure blue-gray.

## Page Chrome

Keep these components consistent:

- Top section tab: horizontal gray navigation bar with the active section in dark blue.
- Bottom brand bar: dark blue strip along the lower edge, often with a cyan angled segment and WuXi AppTec logo at right.
- Blue module header: dark blue rectangle above each content block.
- Page number and sources: small, low-contrast, bottom area.
- Logo placement: usually right side of footer or top right for profile pages.

## Layout Grammar

- Use 16:9 widescreen.
- Use white backgrounds for content pages.
- Use dark-blue full-page backgrounds for cover, chapter divider, and appendix divider pages.
- Build high-density pages from 2-4 structured modules rather than free-floating text.
- Favor grids, columns, tables, matrices, funnels, maps, and timelines.
- Keep borders thin and functional.
- Use icons sparingly as navigational cues or category labels, not decoration.

## Typography

Exact font may vary, but the hierarchy should be stable:

- Main slide title: large, bold, navy, conclusion-first.
- Subtitle: smaller bold or semibold navy/gray, explains the business implication.
- Module header: white text on dark blue.
- Body: compact black or deep gray.
- Source and caveat text: small gray.

Do not use oversized hero typography on dense content pages. Reserve large type for covers and section dividers.

## Image And Logo Treatment

- Use photos only where they carry identification or context: cover image, stakeholder headshots, location photos, or proof points.
- Keep headshots rectangular or simple cropped rectangles.
- Use company logos as identifiers inside profile pages or landscape maps.
- Do not let photos dominate target profile pages; the analysis grid is the main visual system.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- Generic blue corporate slides without the top tabs, footer bar, or module headers.
- Decorative cards that reduce scan density.
- Text-only slides with no chart, table, map, icon, or structured visual.
- Inconsistent page-by-page layout for comparable targets.
- Turning a BD deck into a public marketing deck.
